Features Overview

All essential pregnancy calculations and developmental information – fast, precise, and beautifully presented.

Precise Week & Logbook

Calculate the exact pregnancy week, current month, and standard maternity logbook entry format (e.g., 24 + 5).

Day-by-Day Countdown

Track exactly how many days you have completed and how many days remain until your estimated due date (EDD).

Weekly Info (Weeks 1–42)

Rich medical insights and fetal illustrations for every pregnancy week, including head diameter, weight, and development details.

Time Machine

Explore your pregnancy status for any specific past or future date – perfect for review, doctor appointments, or planning.

Home Screen Widget

Glance at your current pregnancy week and day counter instantly without opening the app via the handy home screen widget.

100% Privacy & Offline

Your due date and health data stay strictly on your local device. No user accounts, no uploads to external servers.
